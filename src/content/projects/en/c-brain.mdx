## What it is for

A command-line agent is brilliant inside a session and amnesiac between two. GreyMatter
gives it memory that lasts: whatever is solved once is distilled into a note, filed,
linked, and found again in the next session.

## What the engine does

- **Distillation**: a working session becomes clean notes — one fact per note, with its
  provenance and its degree of certainty.
- **Recall on demand**: ask — a keyword, a sentence or a command — and the notes that
  answer come back in under 50 milliseconds across a thousand notes. Nothing enters the
  conversation unless it was asked for.
- **Knowledge tree**: notes link to each other; a lesson learned on one project is
  findable from every other.
- **Four maintenance ships**: eight narrow missions — distil, file, challenge, link,
  archive, repair, watch the machine, synthesise — carried by four agents named after the
  ships of *Alien*. Each mission writes only in its own zone.
- **The map and the pill**: the whole trunk as a cloud of points you can walk through,
  and a pill in the menu bar that says which agent is working, and on what.

The public package is at version 1.28: recall there is still automatic and maintenance
still carries eight role names. The pill, the ships and recall on demand ship with the
next release.

## What measurement changed

- **Automatic recall was switched off.** Until 9 September, the notes judged relevant were
  pasted into every question unasked. Over thirty days, only 4% were then opened — 139 out
  of 3,256. The search stayed, the unrequested suggestion went; one setting brings it back
  without touching the code.
- **The pill became light.** At rest it took 28% of a processor core, and another 29% from
  the program that draws the screen: every frame per second was paid for twice. Each
  element now animates at the pace of what visibly moves in it, and nothing draws while
  hidden — at rest, 4% and 20%.

## How it installs and stays alive

- **One command**: the package installs as an extension to a command-line agent.
- **Every release is replayed** by continuous integration: full install, uninstall, and
  every past migration run **twice** — with the requirement that it has eaten no note.
- **46 releases published**, under the Apache 2.0 licence, on a public repository.
