## The problem

DGC SYSTEM technicians logged interventions on paper, later re-typed at the office —
losses, duplicates, delays. The field means unstable network, a gloved hand, harsh daylight:
a consumer-grade app does not survive there.

## What I designed

An **offline-first PWA** built for the job site: fast capture, geolocated photos, an adaptive
questionnaire per intervention type, and office-side PDF report generation. Writes go into a
**queue** that resyncs the moment the network returns — no data lost on a drop or a refresh.

## The impact

The field log becomes the source of truth: no re-typing, an intervention report ready in one
tap, and an office view tracking activity in real time.
